About Yegara Space

A place that takes your work seriously

Yegara Space began with a simple observation: good work needs a good room. Quiet when you need to concentrate, sociable when you want company, and reliable every day of the week.

Our members are founders, consultants, designers, engineers and remote teams. Some take a desk for a day, others have been here for years. The mix is deliberate — introductions happen at the coffee bar more often than at any networking event.
